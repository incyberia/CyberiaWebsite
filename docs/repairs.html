Speed Up Your Computer

Make an old computer feel new again

Most "slow" computers aren't broken — they're just under-specced for what you're asking them to do today. We upgrade the two components that make the biggest difference:

  • More RAM — smoother multitasking, fewer freezes with browser tabs and heavy applications.
  • SSD / NVMe hard disks — dramatically faster boot times and application loading compared to a traditional hard disk.
