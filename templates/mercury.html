Mercury — Imaging & Reporting Software for Radiology

Two purpose-built products for hospitals, diagnostic centres and radiologists: Mercury Image Management for DICOM imaging, and Mercury Reporting for Ultrasound, CT and MR reporting.

🩻

Mercury Image Management Software

A DICOM image management system for storing, retrieving and viewing medical images across your facility.

  • DICOM-compliant image storage & retrieval
  • Fast image search by patient, date or study
  • Built-in viewer for radiologists & referring doctors
  • Works alongside your existing imaging equipment
  • Local installation & support — no dependence on foreign vendors
📝

Mercury Reporting Software

A dedicated reporting tool for Ultrasound, CT and MR studies, built to speed up radiologist workflow.

  • Structured, templated reporting for US / CT / MR
  • Faster turnaround from scan to signed report
  • Customisable report templates per modality
  • Print & digital delivery of reports
  • Designed around how Indian radiology departments actually work
